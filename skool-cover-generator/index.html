    Skool cover FAQ

    What size is a Skool cover image?

    Skool recommends 1084 x 576 pixels (Skool Help Center). This generator exports exactly that, as a PNG. More on the Skool cover image size page.

    Why does my cover matter so much?

    Your group needs a cover image to be eligible for Discovery, and Skool lists "high-quality artwork" as a ranking boost and "low-quality artwork" as a penalty (Discovery FAQs). For paid groups, Skool's Growth Boost ads also use your cover image, About page and pricing (Growth Tab and Skool Ads).

    What makes a good cover?

    One clear promise in 6 words or fewer, strong contrast, a single focal point, text you can read at thumbnail size, and no tiny logos. The 5-point check above tests for these.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. There's no sign-up and no email, and there's no watermark. Your images stay in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
