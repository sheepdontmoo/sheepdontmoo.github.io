Right to request flexible work (not an automatic WFH right)

Under the Fair Work Act, eligible employees can request flexible working arrangements, including working from home. The employer must respond in writing within 21 days. They can refuse only on reasonable business grounds after discussing the request. It is a right to request, not an automatic right to work from home. See the Fair Work Ombudsman's flexible working arrangements page.

So if you need fully remote from day one, it is still faster to apply for roles that are remote already than to negotiate home office after you start.

What Australia-based applicants should check

  1. Anywhere tags first. These employers hire from more than one country. Confirm Australia is allowed and whether you will be an employee or a contractor.
  2. US / UK / EU tags usually need you there. A "Remote, US" or "UK only" role almost always needs you to live in that country with work authorisation. Do not apply from Australia unless the listing explicitly includes Australia.
  3. No Australia region tag yet. Drops use US, UK, EU and Anywhere. Prefer Anywhere, then read each country line carefully.
  4. Hours. Many "worldwide" roles still want overlap with US or UK time. Check core hours against AEST/AEDT before you apply.
  5. Tax and payroll. Australian remote employees need Australian payroll or an employer-of-record. Contractor roles often pay in USD and leave tax to you — ask before you accept.

Australia job-scam signs

The same patterns show up in Australia: fees to "get hired", text-only interviews on Telegram or WhatsApp, fake equipment payments, and task jobs that ask you to top up a balance. Median loss to task scams in BBB Scam Tracker data hit $2,300 in 2025; nearly 50,000 people reported falling victim to an employment scam to BBB over the last three years. (Source: BBB Scam Studies, 'Employment scams 2026 update') Report scams to Scamwatch (ACCC) and, for cybercrime, via the Australian Cyber Security Centre report page. FAQ

Can I work a US or UK remote job from Australia?

Usually not. US- and UK-tagged listings normally need you to live in that country. Look for Anywhere roles that list Australia, or Australian employers hiring remote.

Does your club have an Australia region tag?

Not yet. Drops use US, UK, EU and Anywhere. Australia-based members should prioritise Anywhere leads and check each listing for Australia.

Are these Australia remote jobs free to apply for?

Yes. You apply on the employer's or job board's page. A real employer never charges you to apply or start.

Do I have a legal right to work from home in Australia?

Eligible employees have a right to request flexible working arrangements (including working from home) under the Fair Work Act. The employer must respond, but it is not an automatic right to WFH. See fairwork.gov.au.

Where do I report a job scam in Australia?

Report it to Scamwatch (scamwatch.gov.au). For cybercrime, use cyber.gov.au/report-and-recover/report. If you paid or shared banking details, contact your bank first.
