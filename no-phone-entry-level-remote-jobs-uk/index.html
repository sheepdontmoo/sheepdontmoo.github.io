Confirm what "no-phone" covers

Read the job text for phone duties, customer calls and meeting expectations. A role focused on written work may still involve team calls or occasional phone cover.

Treat "No-phone (likely)" as a reason to investigate further. If the listing is unclear, ask the employer whether phone work is required and how often.

Check UK location and right-to-work requirements

UK-tagged roles usually need you to live in the UK and have the right to work there. Remote describes how you work; it does not automatically remove location restrictions.

Read the employer's requirements before applying. UK employers must check every applicant's right to work before they employ them (GOV.UK: check a job applicant's right to work).

Read the requirements, then prepare your application

Entry-level does not always mean zero requirements. For support, operations, coordination, junior analyst or writing roles, read the duties and required skills before deciding whether the role fits. The title alone cannot tell you whether phone work is involved.

FAQ

Does "No-phone (likely)" mean there will be no calls?

No. It is our reading of the listing, not the employer's promise. Confirm phone duties in the job text and ask the employer if anything is unclear.

Can I apply for UK-tagged remote jobs while living elsewhere?

Check each listing. UK-tagged roles usually require UK residence and the right to work in the UK. Do not assume a remote role permits working from another country.

Can I apply if I have no previous experience?

Read the requirements. Entry-level does not always mean zero experience, qualifications or skills. Use your application to show how you meet the stated requirements.
