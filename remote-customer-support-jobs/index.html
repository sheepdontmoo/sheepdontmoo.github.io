What support ads ask for

When we read real support listings from our drops (for example a product support role at a large cloud-storage company, a US patient support role and a US online chat support role on 10 Nov 2026), the same three things kept coming up:

  1. Clear, fast writing. Friendly, accurate replies by chat and email, often several at once.
  2. Juggling without dropping quality. Handling multiple chats or tickets, keeping notes on every one, and staying calm with upset customers.
  3. Tools and judgment. Help-desk and CRM tools such as Zendesk, Intercom or Salesforce, plus knowing when to escalate an issue instead of guessing.

Some support roles are entry-level; others want several years of high-volume or technical support. Read the requirements and apply where you meet most of them. Put your ticket or chat volume, your tools and one example of a problem you solved in the top third of your CV.

Chat support vs phone support

Leads tagged no-phone (likely) look chat- or email-based from the listing text; calls likely means the listing mentions phone work. It's our reading, not the employer's promise, so confirm in the job text. Many roles also ask you to work set hours in a named time zone.

The fake "chat agent" scam

"Chat moderator, $40/hr, no experience, interview on Telegram" is a classic hook. In 2025, victims reported losses of almost $13 million to AI-involved employment scams; IC3 describes voice spoofing / possible deepfakes in online interviews. (Source: FBI IC3, 2025 Internet Crime Report (AI section)) Find the role on the company's own careers page, and never pay for training or equipment. FAQ

Can I get a remote customer support job with no experience?

Some support roles are entry-level, especially chat and email support, but many want customer-facing experience from retail, hospitality or a call centre. Show that experience clearly on your CV.

Are remote chat support jobs real?

Many are, at real companies. Fake ones promise very high pay for simple chatting, interview only by text on Telegram or WhatsApp, or ask you to pay for training. Check the company's own careers page.

What tools should I know for remote support jobs?

Listings often name help-desk and CRM tools such as Zendesk, Intercom and Salesforce, plus Microsoft Office or Google Workspace. Free trials and tutorials let you learn the basics.

Do remote support jobs need me to live in a certain country?

Usually yes. US, UK and EU tags mean you must live and be able to work there; Anywhere roles accept more than one country, often with set time-zone hours.
