Know what "scam-checked" means

Scam-checked remote job leads have passed automatic checks for warning patterns. Those checks catch patterns, not intent. They cannot prove an employer is genuine or make a listing risk-free.

Every lead includes its source and an apply link. Check the employer and job text before sharing personal information. You apply directly to the employer. Nobody can promise you a job.

Check the region before spending time on an application

Each lead is tagged USA, UK, Europe or Anywhere. Start with the tags that fit where you live, then read the employer's location, working-hours and work-authorisation requirements.

Remote does not always mean you can work from any country. Even an Anywhere tag is a starting point: confirm the employer's requirements in the listing.

Turn the daily drop into a manageable shortlist

The free public pages show a sample. The full daily drop of 10–15 leads and the classroom are inside the free club.

  1. Read the region tags and shortlist suitable roles.

The classroom also has CV and cover-letter templates, ChatGPT prompts, a scam-spotting guide and interview prep.

  • Remote only: hybrid, on-site and "X days in the office" roles are removed.
  • Posts that mention fees, deposits, gift cards, cheques, crypto payments or Telegram/WhatsApp interviews are removed.
  • MLM, "be your own boss" and commission-only listings are removed, plus stale posts and duplicates.
  • Every lead names the employer and the job board it came from, and the apply link is checked when the drop is built.

Automatic checks catch patterns, not intent. Always read our remote job scam checklist before you share ID or bank details, and never pay to apply.
