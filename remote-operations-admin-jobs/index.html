What these ads ask for

When we read three real listings from the 12 Nov 2026 drop (an operations associate at Wynd Labs, a graduate trading operations analyst at Trading212 and a junior analyst at Lendable), the same three things came up:

  1. Attention to detail. Accurate records, invoices and reconciliations, and investigating discrepancies.
  2. Spreadsheets and data. Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Drive) for the operations role; SQL and some Python for the analyst role.
  3. Clear writing. Reports, briefing notes, procedures and recommendations that other people can follow.

Experience levels differ. The Wynd Labs role is early-career (recent graduate or 0 to 2 years in admin, operations, finance or coordination). The Trading212 role is for current or recent graduates. The Lendable role lists 1+ years in an analytical role, so it suits someone with a little experience. Read the requirements and apply where you meet most of them.

CV tip

Put one line of proof in the top third of your CV: the volume you handled (invoices, tickets, records), the tools you used and one process you made better.

FAQ

Can I get a remote operations or admin job with no experience?

Some are early-career: the Wynd Labs operations associate role accepts recent graduates or 0 to 2 years in an admin, operations, finance or coordination role. Others ask for more, so check each listing.

What skills do remote operations jobs ask for?

In the listings we read: attention to detail, spreadsheets and Google Workspace, comfort with numbers and invoices, and clear written communication.

Do remote analyst jobs need SQL?

Often. The Lendable junior analyst listing asks for SQL and working knowledge of Python; entry-level operations roles usually lean on spreadsheets instead.

Do these remote roles need me to live in a certain country?

Usually yes. USA, UK and Europe tags mean you must live and be able to work there; Anywhere roles accept more than one country, often with time-zone hours.
