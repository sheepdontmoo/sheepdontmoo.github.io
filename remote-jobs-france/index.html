What France-based applicants should check

  1. EU tags that list France. An EU-tagged lead means at least one EU country is listed. Confirm France is in the list before you apply.
  2. Other EU-country tags. A role listed only for Poland or Bulgaria is not a France role. You generally need the right to work and be hired in the listed country.
  3. Anywhere tags. These employers hire from more than one country. Confirm France is allowed and whether you will be an employee or a contractor.
  4. US / UK tags usually need you there. Do not apply from France unless the listing explicitly includes France.
  5. Language. Many tech roles are in English; sales, support and admin roles often need French or another language. Check the listing.
  6. Remote terms. Ask how many days, if any, you must be on site and how the employer sets remote work in your contract. Get it in writing.
  7. EU citizens generally don't need a work permit elsewhere in the EU (Your Europe: work permits); payroll setup is still the employer's decision.

France job-scam signs

The same patterns show up in France: fees to "get hired", text-only interviews on Telegram or WhatsApp, fake equipment payments, and task jobs that ask you to top up a balance. Median loss to task scams in BBB Scam Tracker data hit $2,300 in 2025; nearly 50,000 people reported falling victim to an employment scam to BBB over the last three years. (Source: BBB Scam Studies, 'Employment scams 2026 update') For help and to report online fraud, see Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr and the official Internet-signalement.gouv.fr portal. FAQ

Can I get a fully remote job in France?

Yes. Some employers hire fully remote staff in France, other EU-country roles may accept you if France is listed, and Anywhere roles accept applicants from more than one country. Always check the country list.

Does your club have a France region tag?

Not yet. Drops use US, UK, EU and Anywhere. France-based members should prefer EU leads that list France, then other EU leads, then Anywhere.

Do I need to speak French for these jobs?

Not always. Many tech roles work in English, but support, sales and admin roles often need French. The listing says which languages are required.

Are these France remote jobs free to apply for?

Yes. You apply on the employer's or job board's page. A real employer never charges you to apply or start.

Where do I report a job scam in France?

Use Internet-signalement.gouv.fr to report online fraud, and Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr for help. If you paid or shared banking details, contact your bank first.
