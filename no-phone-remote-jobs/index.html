How we tag "no-phone (likely)"

The tag is our reading of the listing, not the employer's promise. A lead is tagged no-phone (likely) when the title is a written, technical or chat-based role (writer, editor, engineer, analyst, designer, QA, data entry, chat or email support and similar) and the listing doesn't mention calls, dialers, inbound/outbound or telesales. Listings that do mention those get a calls likely tag instead. Always confirm in the job text.

Be honest with yourself about this: almost every job has some video meetings. "No-phone" here means no call-centre or phone-sales work, not "never talk to anyone".

No-phone roles that show up most

  • Chat and email support: ticket-based customer support, often entry-level.
  • Writing and editing: technical writing, content, copywriting, proofreading.
  • Data and operations: analyst, data entry, bookkeeping, accounts payable/receivable.
  • Tech: developers, DevOps, QA testers and designers.
  • Trust and safety: content moderation and annotation roles.

Many no-phone roles are skilled (engineering, technical writing, analyst). Entry-level no-phone roles exist but are competitive, so apply early.

The no-phone scam warning

"No phone, no experience, work from your phone" is one of the most common hooks in job-scam adverts, often followed by a text-only "interview" on Telegram or WhatsApp. A real employer will usually still want one video or phone interview before hiring you. In 2025, victims reported losses of almost $13 million to AI-involved employment scams; IC3 describes voice spoofing / possible deepfakes in online interviews. (Source: FBI IC3, 2025 Internet Crime Report (AI section))

FAQ

Are there remote jobs with no phone calls?

Yes. Chat and email support, writing, data, operations and tech roles often have no phone duties. Most still have some video meetings.

What does 'no-phone (likely)' mean?

It's our reading of the job title and text: written, technical or chat-based work with no mention of calls or dialers. Always confirm in the listing.

Is a no-phone job with a text-only interview legit?

Be very careful. Interviews held only by text on Telegram or WhatsApp are a common scam pattern. Check the role on the company's own careers page.

Are no-phone jobs good for introverts?

Many are. Written support, writing, data and tech roles let you work mainly in writing, though most teams still meet by video sometimes.
