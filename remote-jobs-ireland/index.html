So remote work is more normal in Ireland than in most of Europe. The catch is that many roles in the data are hybrid, and many multinational listings that mention Dublin still expect office days. The club only keeps roles that say they are fully remote.

Your right to request remote work in Ireland

Since 7 March 2024, every employee in Ireland has a legal right to request remote working under the Work Life Balance and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2023. An approved arrangement can only start after 6 months' continuous service, the request must be in writing at least 8 weeks before the start date, and the employer must reply within 4 weeks (extendable by up to 8 weeks). The employer can refuse, but must give reasons and follow the Workplace Relations Commission's Code of Practice (WRC Code of Practice, Act, section 20).

That is a right to ask, not a right to remote work. If remote matters to you, it is still faster to apply for roles that are remote from day one.

Reading "Ireland" in a remote listing

  • "Ireland" or "IE" in the location list usually means the employer can hire you on Irish payroll or through an employer-of-record.
  • "EMEA" or "Europe" may or may not include Ireland. Check the listed countries or ask the recruiter.
  • "UK" roles generally need you to live in the UK, even though Ireland is next door. Different payroll, different tax.
  • "Anywhere" roles accept any country, but often work on US or European hours.

Scam signs to watch

The same rules apply as everywhere: never pay to get a job, never interview only by chat app, and never deposit a cheque or move money for an "employer". If you've lost money or shared bank details, contact your bank first, then report it to your local Garda station (An Garda Síochána: fraud). See our full remote job scam checklist.

FAQ

Can I get a fully remote job in Ireland?

Yes. Some employers hire fully remote staff in Ireland, and Anywhere roles accept applicants from any country. Many Irish listings are hybrid, so check the listing says fully remote.

Do I have a right to work from home in Ireland?

Since 7 March 2024 you have a legal right to request remote working under the Work Life Balance and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 2023. An approved arrangement can start after 6 months' service. The employer can refuse with reasons.

Do UK remote jobs accept applicants in Ireland?

Usually not. UK-tagged roles normally require you to live in the UK for payroll and tax. Look for roles that list Ireland, Europe including Ireland, or Anywhere.

Where do I report a job scam in Ireland?

Contact your bank straight away if you paid or shared card details, then report it to your local Garda station.
