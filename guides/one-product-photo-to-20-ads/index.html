One product photo to 20 ad creatives in 7 days (a ChatGPT workflow)

Ads fatigue in 7–10 days. A UGC video costs around $150 at the median (Pitchlo, 154 briefs). If you're a one-person brand, you can't buy fresh creative every week. Here's the workflow I'd use to make 20 test-ready images from one product photo, in under an hour a day.

Day 1: Photo and brand sheet (30 min)

Shoot the product by a window, label to camera. Write four lines: what it is, two real benefits, brand colours, and who buys it and where they use it.

Day 2: 4 product heroes

Two clean packshots and two props-styled heroes. Attach your photo and open each prompt with the preservation line (see the photo-first method).

Day 3: 4 texture and detail shots

Macros of what's inside or what it's made of. These make strong first cards for carousels.

Day 4: 4 lifestyle scenes

Put the product where your customer actually uses it: bathroom shelf, gym bag, desk, picnic.

Day 5: 4 UGC-style hook frames

Phone-selfie style, varied people and rooms. Label them as AI and don't present them as customers.

Day 6: 4 copy versions

Pick 4 renders with empty space and add a hook in Canva. You now have 16 clean images plus 4 with headlines: 20 creatives.

Day 7: Check, label, test

Check Pass if
Product accuracy Label, logo, shape, colour and size match
Hands, faces, text No extra fingers or warped text
Claims No invented reviews or results
Disclosure AI label on where the platform requires it

Then run them at equal budget for a few days and compare thumb-stop and click-through. Keep the winners and make more of the winning angle next week.
