5 phrases that make AI product images look less fake (with before/after)

I rendered 41 ecommerce ad prompts in ChatGPT image generation across 8 product types (skincare, supplements, fashion, food, tech, home, pets and jewellery), each next to a lazy prompt like "a photo of a serum bottle ad". These five phrases did the most work.

1. "true-to-life colour"

Stops the oversaturated, glowing "AI look". Products come out the colour they'd be under real light.

2. "crisp contact shadow"

The single biggest fix for floating products. A defined shadow where the product meets the surface makes it sit in the scene.

3. "label facing camera and perfectly legible"

It doesn't guarantee correct text, but it keeps the label front-on and readable more often. Pair it with a real product photo (see the photo-first method) for best results.

4. "no extra objects"

Without it, models love to add random props, duplicate products and stray leaves. Name the props you do want, then add this.

5. "no retouching, real skin texture" (for UGC-style shots)

People in AI images default to plastic skin. This phrase, plus "front-camera perspective, slightly uneven indoor lighting", gets you something that looks like a phone photo.

Use UGC-style AI images as hook frames or thumbnails, label them as AI, and never present an AI person as a real customer.

Bonus: camera language

"85mm lens, f/8" for packshots, "35mm, f/2" for lifestyle shots. The model has seen millions of captioned photos and responds to the vocabulary photographers use.

Put it together

Candid lifestyle photograph of [your product] in [where your customer uses it], natural daylight, lived-in but tidy, product in sharp focus in the foreground, background softly blurred, looks like a real customer's home and not a catalogue, 35mm lens, f/2. Vertical 4:5.
