Test 5 ad angles with AI statics before you pay for UGC

Small brands often buy UGC videos before they know which angle works. A cheaper order is to find the angle with AI statics first, then brief a real creator only on the winner.

The five angles

  1. Clean hero: the product, beautifully lit.
  2. Texture or ingredient macro: what's inside.
  3. Lifestyle in context: where your customer uses it.
  4. UGC-style hook frame: phone-shot feel, labelled AI.
  5. Offer banner: product on one side, empty space for the offer.

The loop

  • Make one image per angle from your real product photo (photo-first prompts keep the product accurate).
  • Run them as static ads at equal budget for a few days.
  • Compare thumb-stop rate and click-through, not likes.
  • Brief a real creator on the winning angle only.
  • Refresh the losers next week.

Example prompt: offer banner

Minimal ad layout photograph: [your product] placed on the left third on [surface], the right two-thirds is clean empty background in [brand colours] for headline and price text, soft studio light, crisp shadow, no text in the image. Horizontal 1.91:1.

What this won't tell you

Statics test the angle, not the final video. A winning static angle is a strong hypothesis for a video brief, not a guarantee. Results depend on your product, offer and account.
