What "Anywhere" actually means

  1. Not "every country". Many "Anywhere" ads still exclude sanctioned countries, or only hire where they have an employer-of-record. Read the country list.
  2. Often contractor / freelance. You may invoice in USD or EUR and handle your own tax. Ask how they pay and who covers benefits.
  3. Hours still matter. A US company hiring "worldwide" often wants overlap with Eastern Time. Check the listing for core hours.
  4. US / UK / EU tags are different. Those roles need you to live and have the right to work in that region. Don't apply to a US-only role from abroad.

Why Anywhere roles are useful

If you live outside the US, UK or EU hiring hubs, Anywhere roles are often your real pool. They also suit digital nomads and people who move countries. The trade-off is more competition and less job security than a local employee contract.

  • Feb 2026: fully remote jobs were 9% of US paid LinkedIn postings but drew 37% of applications (and 32% of views). (Source: LinkedIn Economic Graph, US Monthly Economic Insights, March 2026)
  • More than 1 in 5 US job seekers exclusively apply to remote jobs (late 2024 data). (Source: LinkedIn Economic Graph, 'The Remote Work Gap', 21 March 2025)

Scam watch for "worldwide remote" ads

Scammers love "work from anywhere, no experience" ads. Median loss to task scams in BBB Scam Tracker data hit $2,300 in 2025; nearly 50,000 people reported falling victim to an employment scam to BBB over the last three years. (Source: BBB Scam Studies, 'Employment scams 2026 update') Never pay to get hired, never interview only on Telegram or WhatsApp, and never cash a cheque for equipment. FAQ

Does Anywhere mean I can apply from any country?

Usually yes, but check the listing for excluded countries and how they hire (employee vs contractor). Some Anywhere ads still need overlap with a US or EU time zone.

Are Anywhere remote jobs usually full-time employment?

Often they are contractor or freelance roles paid in USD/EUR. Ask about contract type, benefits and who handles tax before you accept.

Should I apply to US/UK/EU tags if I live elsewhere?

No. Those tags mean you must live and have the right to work there. Stick to Anywhere, or roles that list your country.

Are these Anywhere jobs free to apply for?

Yes. You apply on the employer's or job board's page. A real employer never charges a fee to apply or start.
