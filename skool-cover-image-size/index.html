Skool cover image size

The Skool cover image size is 1084 x 576 pixels. Skool's Help Center calls it the "community cover image (1084×576 recommended)". Source: Skool Help Center: Growth Tab and Skool Ads.

Why the cover matters (from Skool's Help Center)

  • You need a cover image to be eligible for Skool Discovery, along with a group description and About page description and images (Skool Help Center: Discovery FAQs).
  • Skool lists "high-quality artwork / about page" as a Discovery ranking boost and "low-quality artwork / about page" as a penalty (Skool Help Center: Discovery FAQs).
  • For paid groups, Skool's Growth Boost ads are built from your cover image, About page and pricing. If you turn off AI-generated ads, Skool says it "will only use your cover image as an ad" (Skool Help Center: Growth Tab and Skool Ads).
  • To change it, go to Settings → General → Change next to the group cover photo, then Save (Skool Help Center: group logo and cover photo).

Our tips (not official Skool rules)

  • Keep your words inside the centre 576 x 576 square so a square crop doesn't cut them.
  • Use 6 words or fewer, big and high contrast. Covers are mostly seen at card size.
  • Use one focal point and no tiny logos.
